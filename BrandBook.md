# NERVE Brand Guidelines

**EST 2026 · Alexandria, Egypt**

## Brand Positioning

**NERVE is a contemporary Egyptian concept store built around individuality, movement, and the pieces that become part of your everyday identity. Cool but chic.**

### What We Are

A fashion label designing contemporary streetwear for Egyptian customers who move between local streets and global culture. We design for people who live in Alexandria, Cairo, and beyond — clothing that works for the pace of the city and the rhythm of everyday life.

### What We Stand For

- **Authenticity over hype** — No fabricated heritage, no artificial scarcity beyond real limited drops.
- **Function with intention** — Every design choice serves a purpose: articulated knees for movement, garment-dyed comfort, numbered archive editions.
- **Cairo and beyond** — Rooted in Egyptian dressing today, not aspirational Western trends.

### Who It's For

People aged 18–35 who care about how clothes feel, how they move, and how they show up. They value quality over quantity, design over branding, and pieces that earn their place in rotation.

## Voice & Tone

| Trait                | Description                                                               |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Concise**          | Say it in fewer words. Every sentence must earn its place.                |
| **Confident**        | Not shouting, not apologetic. Steady.                                     |
| **Contemporary**     | Current, not trend-chasing. Fashion-forward without being fashion-victim. |
| **Authentic**        | Grounded in reality. No fake scarcity, no invented heritage.              |
| **Fashion-oriented** | Uses fashion vocabulary precisely (selvedge, garment-dyed, boxy fit).     |
| **Not corporate**    | No business-speak. "Cash on delivery" not "payment upon receipt."         |

### Writing Examples

**Good:** "Numbered archive release. Straight-leg selvedge denim that breaks in to your shape."

**Bad:** "Our denim collection combines premium materials with contemporary design aesthetics."

**Good:** "Lightweight technical shell built for motion — articulated sleeves, taped seams, packable hood."

**Bad:** "This jacket features advanced technical construction for enhanced mobility."

## Product Copy Principles

1. **Lead with a specific detail**, not a vibe. "240gsm cotton" not "premium fabric."
2. **Mention the fit directly.** "Boxy body, dropped shoulder" or "slim crewmead."
3. **Include a distinguishing feature.** "Metal NERVE zip pull," "numbered on the inner waistband."
4. **Keep descriptions under 30 words.** Product cards need scannable copy.
5. **Use the material + construction + detail formula.** E.g., "Water-resistant shell, checkerboard-lined interior, snap-button front and embroidered chest wordmark."

## Editorial Guidelines

### Photography

- No stock filler art. Product photography only.
- Lifestyle imagery should show movement — walking, turning, reaching.
- Natural lighting. Avoid studio-perfect poses.

### Grid & Spacing

- Asymmetric layouts preferred over rigid grids.
- Generous whitespace. Let products breathe.
- Text-to-image ratios should favor the image.

### Social Media Copy

- Reference real products and real people.
- Caption style: brief observation + product detail. E.g., "Seen on the corniche. Archive Denim — 14oz selvedge, numbered edition."
- Never fabricate user-submitted content.

---

## E-commerce Copy

### Cart

- "Your Bag" (not "Shopping Cart") — fashion terminology
- "Added to Bag" confirmation

### Checkout

- Step labels: "Details" → "Shipping" → "Payment" → "Confirm"
- Error states: direct and specific — "Phone number must be 11 digits" not "Invalid input"

### Forms

- Labels above fields
- Helper text kept to one line, max two
- No placeholder-only labels
